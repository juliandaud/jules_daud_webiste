ABOUT

Born in Buenos Aires, Argentina, Jules Daud moved to Minneapolis, Minnesota, where he began playing with different musicians and exploring his musical identity. Those experiences eventually led him to start writing his own songs and developing his solo project.

He later relocated to New York City, where he continues to write, record, and perform, becoming part of the city's independent music scene. His music blends rock & roll, blues, and alternative rock, with lyrics in Spanish.
